What these starting prices cover

One brand, one monthly cycle. Video edits are up to 30 seconds each, using client-supplied footage or agreed existing assets; basic motion, captions and sound finishing are included. Each static creative is a single image. Variants change the hook on an existing approved edit; they are not new original videos. Revisions refine the agreed brief; new concepts are quoted separately.

On-location shoots, talent, travel, paid stock/music licences, complex 3D, ad spend, campaign management and website development are excluded. Taxes, if applicable, and any additional production costs are itemised in the written quote before work starts. No automatic renewal. Final scope, delivery schedule and payment terms are agreed in writing.

05 / A few practical questions

Before
we begin.

Can I start with a consultation?

Yes. Share the challenge, your website and preferred times. I’ll reply to agree the format, timing and any consultation fee before you commit.

Is an appointment confirmed when I send an inquiry?

No. The form prepares an email request. An appointment is confirmed only after we agree a time by email.

Do you shoot original content?

The listed packages start with supplied footage and existing assets. Original filming and production can be scoped separately based on location, talent and deliverables.

Can I hire you for CRO or a website project?

Yes. Website, ecommerce, CRO and creative strategy projects are quoted separately from the content packages.

Do you work with teams outside India?

I’m based in Mumbai and work remotely with teams worldwide. Share your time zone in the inquiry so we can coordinate.

Are results guaranteed?

No. The portfolio figures describe past work. Results depend on your offer, audience, budget, execution and customer experience; we agree the goals and what can be measured before starting.
