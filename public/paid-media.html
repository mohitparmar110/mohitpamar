How I separate tests

Change something worth learning from.

Cosmetic variation has its place. But the strongest learning usually comes from a meaningful difference in what the customer sees, understands or is asked to do.

A
Problem framing

Lead with the pain, tension or need the product resolves.

B
Product truth

Lead with the feature, material, range or service evidence that earns interest.

C
Offer

Test price, delivery, finance or urgency only when the landing experience supports it.

D
Social proof

Use reviews, service evidence and customer signals when trust is the barrier.
