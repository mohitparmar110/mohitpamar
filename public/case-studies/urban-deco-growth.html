Case study · Urban Deco

Improving a furniture store that sells at scale.

Campaign creative, storefront changes, product discovery and paid-media traffic all had to feel like parts of the same retail journey.

RoleCreative / Ecommerce / Growth
FocusShopify, CRO, campaigns
MarketUK & UAE
BusinessFurniture retail

01 / Situation

The job was bigger than making more banners.

The store had a large catalogue, promotional traffic, regional requirements and multiple points where customers could lose clarity. Treating every request as an isolated creative task would only solve symptoms.

02 / Decision

Keep the campaign promise alive after the click.

The creative, collection page, product page, delivery information and offer needed to feel connected. That meant looking at message hierarchy and customer friction at the same time as the visual work.

03 / Work

Storefront changes and campaign work moved together.

The work included campaign assets, PDP and PLP improvements, product-image cleanup, sticky calls to action, collection content, payment messaging and retail motion.

04 / Context

Measured against commercial performance, not asset count.

£1.5M+Annual revenue managed
4.83×PMAX ROAS
£156K+Conversion value
45K+Clicks

These figures describe the commercial environment around the work rather than attributing the result to one isolated design change.
