Case study · Paid creative

Seasonal ad creative built to sell.

A practical approach to paid creative: change something meaningful, keep the campaign idea consistent and make sure the landing page continues the promise.

ChannelsGoogle / Meta
FocusCampaign creative
MethodTesting

01 / Problem

More assets do not automatically mean more learning.

If every version only changes colour, crop or decoration, the campaign may get more files without learning much about what customers actually respond to.

02 / Tests

03 / Work

Build families of assets, not unrelated artworks.

Wide, square, portrait and motion versions share the same core message while adapting to the placement. That makes campaign learning easier to interpret.
