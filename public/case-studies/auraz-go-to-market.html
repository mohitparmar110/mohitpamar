Case study · AuraZ

Launching a jewellery brand with a clearer path to sale.

Product selection, pricing, storefront structure and campaign creative had to be built together from the start.

ScopeBrand launch
PlatformShopify
CategoryJewellery
RoleFounder / Creative / Growth

01 / Situation

There was no existing system to optimise.

The basics had to be decided together: product selection, pricing, collections, presentation, site structure and how the first campaigns would bring people into the store.

02 / Decision

Make the first order believable before overbuilding the brand.

The early work focused on clear pricing, realistic product presentation, useful trust cues and a store structure that made the range easier to understand.

03 / Work

Product, site and campaign were treated as one launch.

Products were renamed and structured, collections were cleaned up, pricing logic was standardised, campaign creatives were produced across formats and early acquisition focused on the strongest categories.

04 / Lesson

Early ecommerce is mostly a prioritisation problem.

When budget and data are limited, the best next move is usually to decide which products, messages and channels deserve attention first rather than trying to do everything at once.
