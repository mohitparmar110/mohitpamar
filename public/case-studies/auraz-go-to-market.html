Case study · Product to market

Building the route
from product
to customer.

A hands-on ecommerce build where sourcing, pricing, positioning, store structure, creative and acquisition decisions had to work together from day one.

ScopeEnd-to-end D2C
PlatformShopify
CategoriesJewellery & accessories
RoleFounder / Creative / Growth

01 / Situation

There was no inherited system to optimise.

The brand needed the commercial basics built together: product selection, price architecture, collection logic, visual identity, product presentation, site structure, promotional planning and the first acquisition approach.

02 / Thinking

Work backwards from what makes the first order believable.

Rather than overbuilding branding before the offer was clear, the work focused on product-market presentation: understandable pricing, trust cues, category structure, realistic lifestyle imagery and a storefront that made the range feel coherent.

03 / Execution

One operating loop across product, site and campaign.

Products were renamed and structured for search intent, collections were reorganised, price and compare-at logic were standardised, campaign creatives were produced in multiple formats, and acquisition planning was tied to the strongest categories rather than spreading budget across the entire catalogue.

04 / Lesson

Early-stage ecommerce is a prioritisation problem.

When budget and data are limited, the most useful strategy is not maximum output. It is deciding which products, messages and channels deserve the next unit of attention and spend.
