I started by making things. Then I got more interested in why they worked.

My background moves through film, post-production, design, ecommerce, performance marketing and CRO. That mix is why I tend to look at the full customer journey instead of protecting one discipline.

I learned by making the work.

Film, editing, motion and design taught me what it takes to turn an idea into something real. That production background still matters because strategy is more useful when you understand what execution actually costs.

Ecommerce made the questions wider.

Once the work sat next to product pages, paid traffic and revenue targets, the questions changed: Which product deserves attention? Does the landing page continue the ad? Where does the customer hesitate? What actually changed after launch?

03

Now I work across the handoffs.

I am comfortable moving between creative direction, Shopify, paid campaigns, CRO, motion and the data behind them. The goal is not to own every discipline — it is to stop the important parts from disconnecting.

04

Tools are useful. They are not the portfolio.

Adobe Creative Cloud, Shopify, GA4, Hotjar/Clarity, Meta, Google Ads and AI production tools are part of the stack. I care more about choosing the right tool for the problem than displaying a long software list.
