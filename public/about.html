My background crosses post-production, design, ecommerce, performance marketing and conversion work. That mix is why I tend to look at the full customer journey instead of protecting one discipline.

Working style

Mumbai · Available for remote and international work

I started with making.

Film, editing, motion and design taught me how ideas are translated into things people actually see and experience. That production background still matters because strategy is more useful when you understand what it takes to execute it.

Then the questions got wider.

Working in ecommerce and growth pushed the work beyond aesthetics: Which products deserve attention? What does the campaign promise? Does the landing page continue that promise? Where does the customer hesitate? Which asset is actually producing value?

Now I connect those layers.

I work best where creative direction, ecommerce, CRO, paid media and production overlap. I can make the thing, but I am more interested in why it should exist, how it should work and what happens after it launches.

Tools are secondary.

Adobe Creative Cloud, Shopify, GA4, Hotjar/Clarity, Meta, Google Ads, AI creative platforms and automation tools are part of the operating stack. The point is not the software list—the point is choosing the right tool for the job.
