AI & systems

Use AI where it saves real time.

I use AI for exploration, production support and repetitive workflow work. The point is not to add AI everywhere — it is to remove unnecessary manual effort without giving up judgement.

Where it helps

Practical use, not prompt theatre.

I care more about the hours saved and the quality kept than whether a workflow sounds futuristic.

01

Creative production

Concept expansion, visual exploration, pre-visualisation, versioning and production support when the workflow needs speed.

02

Workflow automation

Reduce repetitive handoffs between content, ecommerce, analytics and campaign operations.

03

Reusable systems

Turn repeated tasks into a process the team can use again, instead of relying on one clever prompt.

Rule

If AI makes the work faster but worse, it has not helped.

Automation should remove repetition while keeping the decision-making, brand judgement and final quality under human control.
