Creative strategy

Decide what the work needs to say before deciding how it should look.

I use strategy to make the brief clearer: who we are talking to, what matters, what proof we have and what the customer should understand next.

PositioningCampaignsTestingAfter the click

01

Start with the customer problem, not the moodboard.

What is the customer trying to solve? What does the business need them to notice? What makes the offer believable? Those questions usually matter more than generating another visual direction.

One main message

Decide what deserves the first second of attention.

Proof that matters

Use product, service and customer evidence to support the claim.

02

Build one idea that survives more than one format.

A campaign should still feel like the same thought when it moves from a Meta ad to a landing page, homepage banner, video or email.

03

Test something worth learning from.

If two ads only change colour, the learning is limited. I prefer testing a real difference: hook, proof, offer, product angle or the promise carried into the landing page.

04

The click is not the finish line.

Strong creative can still lose the customer if the page becomes confusing. The message, offer, product information and next action need to stay connected after the click.
