How I think about motion

Movement should change how the message is understood.

If the same idea works just as well as a still, motion should not be added for the sake of motion.

01
Attention

Use movement to earn the first moment without sacrificing clarity.

02
Product

Reveal scale, material, detail or use in a way a single frame cannot.

03
Offer

Control timing so promotional information lands in the right order.

04
Channel

Adapt pacing and framing to where the work will actually be seen.
